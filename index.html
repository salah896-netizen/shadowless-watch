Where the numbers come from. Prices are PriceCharting's monthly values for each PSA grade, which track completed eBay sales. Snapshot taken 7 Nov 2026, shown every two months from Nov 2021. Conversions use US$1 = £0.74 = A$1.44, eBay's rates on 7 Nov 2026. These are market estimates, not quotes.
